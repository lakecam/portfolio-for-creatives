Monty

Personal context manager

A personal context management tool that aids in the capture, storage, application, and distillation of the context pieces needed in knowledge work.

Role

Solo UX Design

Timeline

May – Aug 2026

Status

Concept

Context

Mentor-Led Project

Platform

Mac Native App

Problem

Knowledge workers switch between 13 apps, 30 times daily

Knowledge work is spread across many apps, and workers spend nearly an hour looking for information. The issue is where things are stored, not how focused people are.

Audience

Knowledge workers who need files scattered across apps

They engage in work that spans research, synthesis and execution, and rely on maintaining context across complex, interconnected information to execute their tasks.

Scenario

Why did we pivot to this layout?

You're in a meeting and you get asked this dreaded question. You know the answer, but it’s scattered across your computer.

PDF in one folder
Feedback in Slack from three weeks ago
Screenshots on your desktop

Three minutes of frantic click scrambling ensues, momentum is lost and stakeholder trust reduces. This occurs weekly and each scramble has a 15 minutes recovery period.

Status quo

1 workflow,
5 tools

Each job has a specialist app. Every handoff between tools leaves context in another place.

Reflection

Reviewing other apps and testing Heptabase’s canvas showed that saving things is easy and keeping them organised is the real work. Every folder or canvas a tool adds is one more thing to maintain. For the same reason, Monty’s shortcut avoids the keys Save As and Spotlight already use.

Test plan

Test a prototype with 3–5 knowledge workers. Compare time to find a saved item with their usual Mac folders.
