Monty

Personal context manager

Filing takes longer than capturing, so Monty saves from any app in 1 shortcut and finds things by meaning, not filename.

Audience

Knowledge workers who need files scattered across apps

They engage in work that spans research, synthesis and execution, and rely on maintaining context across complex, interconnected information to execute their tasks.

Scenario

Why did we pivot to this layout?

You're in a meeting and you get asked this dreaded question. You know the answer, but it’s scattered across your computer.

PDF in one folder
Feedback in Slack from three weeks ago
Screenshots on your desktop

Three minutes of frantic click scrambling ensues, momentum is lost and stakeholder trust reduces. This occurs weekly and each scramble has a 15 minutes recovery period.

Problem

Knowledge workers switch between 13 apps, 30 times a day

Knowledge work is fragmented across channels and workers spend nearly an hour looking for information. This is not a focus problem. It's an infrastructure problem.
