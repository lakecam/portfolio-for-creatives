Monty

Personal context management

A Mac app that saves screenshots, files and notes with 1 shortcut, finds them by meaning instead of filename, and shows how they connect.

Role

Research, UX and UI

Jul 2026

Audience

Knowledge workers whose work spans research, synthesis and execution, and who rely on context spread across many apps.

Problem

Context is scattered across apps

Knowledge work is split across apps and channels, which makes lost context an infrastructure problem, not a focus problem.
