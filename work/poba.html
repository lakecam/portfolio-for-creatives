Poba

Making friends via board games

An app that turns board game nights into real friendships. Players find or host a night nearby, and after each one, those who want to play again become a regular group.

Role

Research, UX and UI

Timeline

Sep to Dec 2025, revised Fall 2026

Status

Concept, not yet tested

Platform

Mobile app

Young adults (18–29) who want genuine friendships without the small talk and are open to making them through structured board-game nights with like-minded people.

Problem

Friendship apps stop at small talk

Most friendship apps stop at coffee chats and shared interests, too shallow to turn strangers into friends (IMARC Group, 2025).

First Idea

Matching by personality

The first version matched players by personality before each night, to make meeting strangers less awkward.

Pivot

Interviews ruled out personality matching

Finding

In 4 interviews, personality matching read as a gimmick rather than a real need.

Change

Groups now form around how people like to play, and 1 question after each night keeps good groups together.

Art Direction

From Greek to nostalgic play

Poba started as Zari, Greek for dice, so the first look drew on ancient Greek art. However, this felt contrived, so a brighter look based on '90s party games replaced it to tap into nostalgia instead

Iteration

Asking 1 question, not rating players

Rating each player felt like ranking people, which hurt quieter players most, so everyone answers 1 question instead.

Looking back

Drop a theme that doesn't help

Reflection

Dropping the ancient Greek theme late was stressful, yet the work moved faster once the look matched the feel of a fun night in.

Test plan

Moderated sessions with groups of 4 to 8, tracking how many say they'd play again and actually book the next night.
