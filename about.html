  • About

    Lake Cam is a product designer from Borneo, Malaysia, now based in Vancouver. He led design system and features design at Westland Insurance, with a specific interest in aligning design standards across product, branding and marketing.

    Equally interested in high-level strategic decisions and granular pixel work, he looks at everything through systems thinking, so design, technology and business stay in a collaborative, symbiotic relationship.

    Outside of work, he enjoys spending time with his partner, Ben, and his three dogs, eagerly awaiting the latest episode of Survivor, playing social strategy board games every weekend, and eating nonstop momos.
