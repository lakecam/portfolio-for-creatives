  • About

    I’m Lake Cam, a product designer from Borneo, Malaysia, now based in Vancouver. I led design system and features design at Westland Insurance, specifically in aligning design standards across product, branding and marketing.

    Equally interested in high-level strategic decisions and granular pixel work, I have a deep fascination for design systems, and creating modular scalable interfaces. Recently, I’ve been super into the indie development scene, and the idea of making software that feels homemade.

    Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my partner, Ben, and our three dogs, eagerly awaiting the latest episode of Survivor, playing social strategy board games every weekend, and eating nonstop momos.
