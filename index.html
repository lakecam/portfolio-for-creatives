About Me

Lake Cam is a product designer and design engineer from Malaysia, now based in Canada. With 3+ years of experience in crafting design systems and enterprise software, he has also been super into the indie dev scene and the idea of making software that feels human and homemade.

Outside work, he is also an avid historian of 1990s–2010s pop culture, a massive Survivor and Big Brother fan, casually thinks about rowing (and maybe doing it) and has a deep interest in human geography and Southeast Asian history.
